Adam Carter‑Groves

I design the systems that give games their depth and keep players coming back: progression, economy, metagame and AI.

9 years in game design and 6 years as a developer before that, so I'm as happy building a system in Unreal, Unity or Godot as I am writing the design doc for it.

What I design

Systems, from the first model to the final tuning pass

Progression & economy

Reward loops, currencies and unlock curves, modelled before they're built and tuned once players get their hands on them.

Worms Rumble · Speedball · Honey, I Joined a Cult · Steel City Interactive

Metagame & structure

The layer around the core game that gives each session a reason to exist: campaigns, missions and long-term goals.

Speedball · Achtung! Cthulhu Tactics · Mars Horizon · Astronimo

AI & combat

Opponents and combat systems that are readable, fair and fun to play against, implemented in engine with behaviour trees.

Speedball · Achtung! Cthulhu Tactics · Worms Rumble

In engine & on paper

A developer background means I prototype and implement my own designs, and keep the documentation clear enough for the whole team to build from.

Unreal · Unity · Godot · Blueprints · C# · Design documentation · Mentoring
